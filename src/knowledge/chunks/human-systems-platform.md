# Human Systems Platform

Human Systems Platform is Dessi's founder product for AI-augmented career and capability development. It learns from selected human-AI work, represents capability through evidence, helps individuals explore jobs, collaborators, funding, markets, and next capabilities, and helps organisations discover relevant talent.

AI can now produce convincing code, applications, portfolios, assessments, and interview answers at very low cost. The finished output says less about how the work happened. It does not show who understood the problem, what was delegated, how failure was detected, what changed through learning, or whether the capability will survive a new situation.

The platform turns selected episodes of human-AI work into private memory and learning, owner-approved evidence, and bounded hypotheses about where that capability may create value. Its Opportunity Studio researches the demand, alternatives, contradictions, and opportunities around a disclosed profile, project, or product. Later outcomes are intended to challenge or improve the next cycle.

## Who It Is For

The first individual users are AI-native builders, researchers, designers, engineers, and technical operators. They need a way to learn from work and demonstrate capability without surrendering their private history.

The first organisational users are smaller AI-native companies, specialist recruiters, hiring managers, and technical leaders. They need better evidence about how someone works with AI in the conditions of a real role or problem.

The initial commercial hypothesis is evidence-backed talent discovery and capability development. Human reviewers receive evidence, context, limitations, and a challengeable transfer hypothesis. They do not receive a score or an automated employment decision.

The product supports both exploitation and exploration. Exploitation connects evidenced current capability to immediate roles and organisational demand. Exploration researches adjacent roles, collaborators, project funding, markets, and capabilities the person could develop. The public profile network is intended to make owner-approved evidence discoverable by people and agents on both sides: organisations looking for talent and individuals looking for work, collaborators, or support for a new project.

## How the Products Fit Together

- **Dessi Space** is the first deployment of the private Personal Space module. It preserves selected experience, provenance, reflection, and the owner's private decisions.
- **Learning Foundry** is the human-agent learning module. It develops human understanding and separately governed agent capabilities.
- **Human Systems Platform** carries identity, evidence, context, review, publication authority, and later correction across the product boundaries.
- **Opportunity Studio** is the developing Personal Space research module. It uses an explicitly disclosed profile and project snapshot to investigate direct, adjacent, developmental, and exploratory opportunities as well as product-market questions.
- **DG-OS** is the reusable public expression and discovery module. Dessi's profile is its first live instance, and only owner-approved material may enter it.
- **Organization Foundry** is the planned product for describing the work, constraints, hazards, and capability demand on the organisational side.

Each product has value on its own. They exchange bounded records through reviewed transitions instead of sharing one private database.

## Current State

The private working repository contains shared evidence contracts and tested components for owner review, exact approval, publication signing, protected local persistence, Dessi Space proposals, Learning Foundry evidence, DG-OS publication preparation, and a deterministic Opportunity Studio preview. The preview validates an approved research mission and disclosure-safe profile before accepting cited observations, opportunity or venture-market candidates, four-lane hypotheses, coverage, and owner decisions. Research remains manually invoked and cannot act externally for the owner.

The first dogfood research also exposed a design gap: the system must resolve the owner's relevant projects and product documentation before it designs a market mission. Portfolio-aware mission design remains a planned next slice.

The federation is still under development. The complete private-to-public workflow, receiver-observed activation, later-outcome feedback, Organization Foundry, customer pilots, and commercial validation remain incomplete or planned. The public component repositories support the product direction, but they do not prove product-market fit or beneficial employment outcomes.

## Open Source and Customisation

Learning Foundry is open source under MIT. DG-OS software is open source under AGPL-3.0-only, while its name, visual identity, personal profile content, CVs, writing, and media remain outside that software licence. These modules provide inspectable, adaptable learning and public-expression surfaces instead of forcing every person or organisation into Dessi's implementation.

Human Systems Platform, Dessi Space, and Opportunity Studio remain private development and are not described as open source. The separation is intentional: customisable software must not make a person's private evidence public.
