# AI Knowledge Hub Harness

Harness is the practical engineering companion to the independent AI Knowledge Hub publication. It connects system design, algorithms and data, software production, and AI engineering to marketing, growth, and go-to-market work.

Guides and interactive visualisers explain deterministic operations such as retrieval, routing, ordering, and deduplication. Reference architectures and implementation examples state the scope of their evidence. The companion Skills catalogue offers reusable packages and tools that readers can inspect and adapt.
