# AI Harness Lab

AI Harness Lab is a public, open-source learning environment for people building applied AI in marketing. It exists to make the system around a model visible and practical, rather than treating prompts or model choice as the whole product.

The current lab uses interactive examples to explain deterministic structures behind retrieval, routing, ordering, deduplication, memory, permissions, orchestration, and audit. Marketing examples connect each structure to campaign, content, reporting, analytics, and martech work.

The project complements the AI News Hub and AI Skills Platform. Articles explain why a pattern matters, the skills catalog packages reusable capabilities, and the lab lets people inspect how system elements interact. The live lab is functional, but its planned pattern and case-study library remains incomplete and development is selective.
