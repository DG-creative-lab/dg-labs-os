# Performics Labs AI News Hub

**Classification**: Open-source public platform developed in a professional innovation context

**Status**: Active and public

**Site**: https://ai-news-hub.performics-labs.com/

**Repository**: https://github.com/ai-knowledge-hub/performics_labs_ai_news

## The Product

Dessi conceived, built, and maintains the AI News Hub as a shared place for applied AI research in marketing. It helps practitioners, strategists, and engineers move past product announcements and ask practical questions: what changes the work, what becomes measurable, what breaks, and what teams need to build next.

The initial purpose was to create an internal community of thinkers, builders, and innovators. Dessi reports that the community has grown to about 400 company members. That membership figure is owner-reported because public membership and engagement analytics are not available.

## How It Works

The platform joins four forms of work:

- AI News provides short explanations of relevant model, product, protocol, and channel changes.
- Analysis connects those changes to marketing architecture, operating models, and implementation choices.
- All-Hands pages turn research threads into build sessions, reference packs, and working artifacts.
- Public projects carry selected ideas into repositories, prototypes, and practical tools.

This structure gives the community a route from awareness to discussion and implementation.

## Public Evidence

The reviewed repository contains an MIT-licensed Astro application with React and MDX content. At the reviewed state it contains 43 news entries, 19 analyses, six All-Hands sessions, and 109 commits.

The platform demonstrates product development, editorial-system design, technical synthesis, and the ability to connect research with working systems.

## Boundaries

The live site and repository verify the platform and its content structure. Internal membership and engagement figures remain owner-reported. Public bylines may represent collaborative newsroom work, so Dessi's platform development should remain separate from claims of sole authorship for every article.
