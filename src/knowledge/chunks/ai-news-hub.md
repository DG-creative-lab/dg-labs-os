# AI Knowledge Hub

AI Knowledge Hub is Dessi Georgieva’s independent publication. It covers AI in marketing, growth, and go-to-market through research, essays, practical guides, and experiments. Its library includes new work and revised editions of Dessi’s earlier articles.

## Practical companions

- **Skills** at https://skills.ai-knowledge-hub.org/ is the open catalogue of reusable skills, agent templates, plugins, and tools. Each entry states its readiness, setup requirements, and limits.
- **Harness** at https://harness.ai-knowledge-hub.org/ is the practical engineering companion. Guides and interactive visualisers cover system design, algorithms and data, software production, and AI engineering.
